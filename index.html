Theory
Friction is a contact force opposing relative motion between surfaces.

F = μN
F = friction force (N)
μ = coefficient of friction
N = normal force (N)

Static friction prevents motion onset.
Kinetic friction opposes sliding motion.

Theory
Friction enables everyday tasks: writing on paper, walking without slipping, vehicle braking, and climbing. In engineering, friction is harnessed in clutches, brakes, conveyor belts, and mechanical fasteners.

✓ ADVANTAGES

  • Enables walking & running
  • Allows vehicle braking
  • Holds screws & bolts
  • Powers clutch mechanisms
  • Enables writing/grip

✗ DISADVANTAGES

  • Causes energy loss (heat)
  • Wears machine parts
  • Reduces efficiency
  • Increases fuel consumption
  • Requires lubrication
